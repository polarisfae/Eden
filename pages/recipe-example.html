My Grandmother's Plum & Ginger Jam

planted 2 September 2023 · deeply rooted
recipespreservingfamily

This one's older than any other page in the garden, in the sense that it predates the garden itself by about forty years. My grandmother never measured the ginger — this is my best reconstruction, tested three summers running.

you'll need

1.5 kg ripe plums, halved and stoned
800 g sugar
60 g fresh ginger, peeled and finely grated
juice of 1 lemon

method

  1. Layer the plums and sugar in a large bowl, cover, and leave overnight — this draws out the juice and saves you scorching the fruit later.
  2. Tip everything into a wide, heavy pot with the ginger and lemon juice. Bring to a boil slowly, stirring so nothing catches.
  3. Simmer at a steady bubble for 25–35 minutes, until it holds its shape on a cold spoon.
  4. Pot into warm, sterilized jars while still hot. Seal, and let it sit at least a week before opening — the ginger mellows.
She always said it wasn't done until the kitchen smelled like it, not by the clock.
