Wrote this down on a walk and didn't want to lose it: every plant here doesn't need a moral. A seedling is allowed to just be two leaves and a stem, with no idea yet what it's going to become.

I think I've been treating my own notes app like a blog for years — trying to make every entry a finished little essay. This page is what happens if I stop doing that.
