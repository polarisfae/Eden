how it grows

Not a blog. A garden.

Most blogs are timelines — newest on top, everything older buried beneath. Here, every page is a small living thing: a thought, an interest, a note, a photo, a recipe — planted on its own, tended when it's ready, and left to grow at its own pace. Some entries are seedlings, barely a sentence long. Others have had years to climb a trellis. Wander the beds below, or just follow whatever's blooming.
